Comprehensive rehabilitation at the 20-bed gastroenterology department

June 10, 2026 Departments

This is a demo news item — it will be replaced with real content soon.

The center's gastroenterology department has 20 beds and provides comprehensive rehabilitation for children with gastrointestinal diseases.

Rehabilitation is not only medication — it combines diet, regimen, movement and treatment. Individual nutrition, physiotherapy and LFK sessions are organized for patients.

The department's goal is to normalize digestion, reduce pain and prevent relapses.
