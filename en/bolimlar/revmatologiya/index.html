Rheumatology

Early diagnosis, treatment and rehabilitation of rheumatological diseases — since 1972.

1972 established

The Rheumatology department has been operating since 1972, providing specialized care to patients with musculoskeletal and connective tissue diseases. Its main activity is early detection of rheumatological diseases, diagnosis using modern methods, treatment, dynamic monitoring and rehabilitation. Special attention is paid to preventing complications, improving patients' quality of life and reducing disability.

What we treat

  • Rheumatoid arthritis
  • Reactive arthritis
  • Systemic lupus erythematosus
  • Vasculitis
  • Gout
  • Other rheumatological diseases

Department facilities

  • Reception and examination rooms
  • Treatment and procedure rooms
  • Day hospital
  • Laboratory testing facilities
  • Physiotherapy and rehabilitation services
  • Use of splints and orthopedic devices

Treatment and rehabilitation methods

  • Drug therapy
  • Procedures on BTL physiotherapy equipment
  • Magnetotherapy
  • Laser therapy
  • Electrophoresis
  • Ultrasound therapy
  • Paraffin and ozokerite treatment
  • Massage and therapeutic gymnastics (LFK)
