Reception & Diagnostics

An accurate diagnosis is the first step of proper rehabilitation.

The Reception and Diagnostics department specializes in patient reception and diagnostic examination. It performs X-ray diagnostics, ultrasound, EEG, ENMG, EchoEG, ECG and laboratory diagnostic tests. The department also employs a top-category dentist, an otolaryngologist, a pediatrician, a top-category laboratory physician, an ultrasound physician, a cardiologist and a top-category neurologist for EEG and ENMG diagnostics.

What we treat

  • Patient reception and triage
  • Primary and repeat examination
  • Specialist physician consultations

Department facilities

  • X-ray room
  • Ultrasound room (Sonoscape S22, Esaote MyLab X6)
  • EEG and ENMG room
  • ECG and EchoEG room
  • Clinical diagnostic laboratory
  • Dentist and ENT rooms

Treatment and rehabilitation methods

  • X-ray diagnostics
  • Ultrasound examination
  • EEG — brain activity
  • ENMG — neuromuscular diagnostics
  • ECG and EchoEG
  • Laboratory tests
